### Autopilot-full

**You own the verdicts, never the PRs. One owner runs each PR from build to merge, and nothing merges without your clean swarm verdict.** For "autopilot this queue", "full autopilot", and one-owner-per-PR programs. Orchestrate runs a standing program whose coordinator lands verified work itself and whose workers never merge. Here each PR's owner carries the whole lifecycle through the merge, and the root keeps only verification, countersigns, and audits.

1. **Mark the operator's items and honor state-then-wait.** Items the operator names stay hers. She reviews and she clicks, and no owner merges one. When she asks for the protocol or the plan to be stated, deliver the statement and stop. Execution starts only on her explicit go. On that go, use `create_goal` with the full program objective. Read `get_goal` before updating its exact id and revision. Resume an existing goal only on the human's request, and complete it only after the queue is verified done.
2. **Spawn one owner per PR with the full lifecycle and an early trail.** Resolve the forge once for the program. GitHub CLI, `gh`, is the default. If `command -v origin` succeeds and Origin can resolve the repository, use `origin pr ...` for PR create, edit, view, watch, and merge operations. Otherwise stay on `gh` and record the fallback. Never require Graphite. One `pstack_spawn` owner with `role: poteto-agent` per PR owns build, the first push, a ready PR, self-proof on the real artifact per **principle-prove-it-works**, skeptical review triage per `../references/review-triage.md`, `/unslop`, `/no-comments`, a rebase onto current trunk, babysit to green per `playbooks/babysit.md`, and the merge itself. Within about 15 minutes, every owner starts a `decisions.tsv` trail per **show-me-your-work**, pushes its first branch snapshot, and opens the PR ready, never draft. Open the PR before self-proof so the URL, decisions, and checks form a durable trail. Keep `decisions.tsv` uncommitted and return it with the reports. The rebase always precedes babysit and never waits for drift or conflicts. The merge is the one step an owner may not take alone. Step 4 gates it.
3. **Run owners in true parallel and never stack.** Many owners at once when PRs are self-contained, with one writer per branch, disjoint files, and cross-PR drift absorbed by rebase. Only genuinely overlapping work serializes. Self-contained PRs branch straight off main, and sequenced work is merge-then-branch. An owner that must split a genuinely dependent change may hold a short private base-branch stack.
4. **Swarm-verify every merge-ready head before its merge.** At the owner's merge-ready head SHA, fan out parallel independent verifiers per **swarm** and aggregate to one verdict. Re-run the gates at that SHA. Prove the load-bearing behavior live on the real surface the change touches, using browser, CLI, or tests as the change demands. Audit the receipts and the diff, distrusting the PR body. Run the same load-bearing scenario on current trunk. If trunk does not have the feature, record that fact and gate the behavior the diff adds plus the end state the user waits for. The live lane is the floor, and a verdict without it is not clean. No merge without the root's clean verdict. Findings go back to the owner for fix-forward, and the new head gets a fresh swarm and a fresh verdict.
5. **On a clean verdict the owner merges and takes the next item.** The owner merges only from a head freshly rebased onto trunk. The merge-ready report is made at a trunk-current head, and the swarm verdict pins that SHA. If trunk moves again before the merge, the patch-id rule in `playbooks/shipping.md` governs re-verification. A new head voids the verdict unless the patch-id is unchanged. The owner squash-merges its own PR through the resolved forge and picks up its next self-contained item. The operator's full-autonomy grant plus the root's clean verdict is the merge authorization that babysitting alone never has. Operator-named items stop at merge-ready and wait for her click.
6. **Run the root layer.** A genuinely new raise of a pinned gate or budget value needs your fresh countersign, granted only after verifier proof. Absorbing values that already landed on main is drift, not a raise. Audit owners at each goal continuation and completion notification. DSH goal continuation is not a timed scheduler. Do not promise a 30-minute wake. At each audit, re-read this installed playbook and `get_goal`, then check the operation against both. Fix drift during that audit. Probe owners through `list_agents` and their durable reports without sending a resume merely to check status. Count side effects as progress, including commits, pushes, PR or check deltas, and store reports. Investigate a lane that exceeds its expected runtime without a side effect. Confirm it has stopped writing before assigning its files to a replacement. Collect decision trails. When merges batch, run a retro pass and a post-merge bot-comment sweep.
7. **Stand down on the operator's stop.** Send every owner a zero-writes order immediately and request interruption where needed. Read `get_goal`, then pause that exact revision with `update_goal` on the human's stop request. Owners hold their briefs until she releases them.

**Reply:** the queue with each PR's owner, state, and head SHA. Each verdict and the swarm that produced it. What merged and what each owner took next. Countersigns granted and why. Open operator gates. Where the collected decision trails live.
